We caught this one on the very first prototype of our AI onboarding agent in WhatsApp, the one that registers users. The language model did not know today's date, so it blocked registration for people who had just turned 18 and for those who were a month or so older than that, even though they were eligible to sign up. I already knew that language models don't reliably know the current date, so I wrote a test aimed at exactly that boundary: a user who has just turned 18 must be able to register. The test failed on the prototype, which is how the bug showed up.
