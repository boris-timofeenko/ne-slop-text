One of the most valuable tests I've written as a QA tech lead caught a bug that no one would have thought to look for. Our fintech product has an AI onboarding agent in WhatsApp that registers new users, and on its first prototype the LLM didn't know today's date. Because of that, it refused registration to people who had turned 18 only about a month earlier. I knew that language models don't reliably know the current date, so I wrote a test for exactly that age boundary, and it failed on the prototype. It's a good reminder that when you test AI systems, you have to think about what the model fundamentally can't know, and that's why we also check the agent's answers with LLM-as-a-judge.
