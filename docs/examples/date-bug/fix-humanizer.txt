One of the best tests I've written as a QA tech lead caught a bug nobody would have thought to look for. Our fintech product has an AI onboarding agent in WhatsApp that registers new users. On the first prototype the LLM didn't know today's date, so it refused to register people who had turned 18 only about a month earlier. Language models don't reliably know the current date. I knew that, so I wrote a test right on that age boundary, and the prototype failed it. With AI systems you have to think about what the model can't know, and that's why we also check the agent's answers with LLM-as-a-judge.
