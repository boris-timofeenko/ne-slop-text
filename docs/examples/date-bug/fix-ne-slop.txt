I had to test the very first prototype of the AI agent that registers new users in WhatsApp for our fintech product. I know that a neural net doesn't always know what today's date is. If someone has only just turned 18, they should already be able to register, but the neural net didn't know the exact date and blocked registration for people a little over 18, by about a month. So I wrote a test that would fail on exactly that, and on the prototype it failed.
