I'm a QA tech lead at a fintech, and one of our products is an AI onboarding agent that registers users in WhatsApp. When we tested its very first prototype, I already knew one thing about language models: they don't reliably know what today's date is. So I wrote a test for the age boundary, using someone who had turned 18 about a month earlier and should be able to sign up without any trouble. The test failed. The model didn't know the current date and refused registration to people who were already old enough. We also check the agent's answers with LLM-as-a-judge, but this bug was caught by a plain boundary test, written because I knew where models tend to slip.
