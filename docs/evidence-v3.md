# Evidence for v3 (25–26 November 2026)

All texts were written by Sonnet from a bare request plus a description of the reader. Sixteen tasks, mostly
Russian: posts for Telegram, Threads and LinkedIn, blog and newsletter pieces, one announcement, one greeting. The
only evidence counted here is blind reading by one person (Boris), on shuffled sheets with the key hidden.

## What was compared

- Base: 4–8 mandatory searches, then write (later with a word limit).
- Method, content stage: default texts from a local model, a "canon" of what the first obvious searches return,
  digging past it, scoring finds and candidate points for expectedness with token probabilities of a local model,
  a per-sentence gate.
- Method, form stage: the same content written afresh from four pieces by one author.

## Results by sheet

| Sheet | Tasks | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4, base vs method (equal length) | method 2, base 1, neither 1 |
| 2 | 3 tuned tasks, four forms | Chekhov example best in 2, letter-without-example best in 1 and worst in 1, plain method never best |
| 3 | 3 new tasks | base 1, method 1, tie 1 |
| 4 | 3 new tasks, four variants | base best in 2 and worst ("sloppy") in 1; base plus form 1st, 4th, 4th; full method 2nd–3rd |
| Re-read | 4 tasks from sheets 2 and 4, new order | he matched himself in 14 pairs of 21 |

Model judges (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus) had preferred the method 10 votes of 12 on sheet 1's tasks and called the base
"generic" 12 times of 12. The person did not.

## What held

- Search before writing, a length limit, and a coherent telling. The reader's remarks across all sheets were about
  one thing: a lost thread, a disjointed text, an abrupt opener, huge quotes, an ending weaker than the opening.
- The form stage by Chekhov's letters: best in two tasks on both readings. On new tasks on top of the base it was
  first once and last twice, inside the noise.
- The author of the example decides. Doroshevich gave clipped one-line paragraphs and lost the facts; Gilyarovsky
  gave a dry list; Teffi gave irony and aphorisms. Two agents left to choose both took Chekhov.
- Requester-only knowledge cannot be researched. An announcement for an unnamed shelter stated general practice as
  the shelter's own rules; the reader preferred the base.

## What did not

- The content stage with a local model: no gain over the base on six new tasks, at five to eight minutes a text.
- A coherence measure from paragraph order under a local model: highest for the template text, because a template
  has a predictable structure.
- Bans on constructions: the version that forbade contrast pairs opened with one.
- A judge fitted to the reader: three briefs on three models against 44 "better than" pairs. Outside the examples
  the best got 15 of 21, the rest were at chance; a judge given six ranked tasks as examples repeated them without
  error and got 10 of 18 on a new sheet. On the 14 pairs the reader reproduced himself, judges got 5 to 10.
- A stronger writer for the form stage (Fable instead of Sonnet): one place higher in two tasks of three.

## Limits

One reader, sixteen tasks, one writing model. English was checked on two tasks by the author of the method only;
there the form stage changed little. The rules for the opening, quotes and ending were derived from the reader's
remarks and checked blind only as part of method variants, not on top of the base.
